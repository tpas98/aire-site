## CRITICAL CAN DESCRIPTION (include in every prompt)
Use this block as the base description — copy it into each prompt:

> This is a small, round, flat pouch tin — the same shape and size as a Zyn nicotine pouch can. It is NOT a tall beverage can. The form factor is a shallow plastic puck: approximately 65mm diameter, 22mm tall, with a rounded edge and a hinged snap-open lid on top. Think shoe polish tin or Altoids Smalls container.
>
> THE LID (what you see from above):
> - Clean white plastic circular body with a thin rim
> - Small lid-catch notch/tab at the top edge
> - Across the top curved edge of the label: "NO NICOTINE. NO CAFFEINE. PURE BALANCE" in small dark navy uppercase text, arched along the rim
> - Below that, small text: "Dietary Supplement"
> - Center of the lid: the word "aire" in dark navy lowercase serif font (DM Serif Display style), large and prominent, with a subtle flowing wave/breath mark in light cyan-blue just above and to the right of the "a"
> - Below "aire": the tagline "FIND YOUR BALANCE" in dark navy uppercase sans-serif bold letters, with a thin horizontal line above it
> - Lower portion of lid: "15 Calm Mint Pouches" in navy bold, then below in lighter text "Rhodiola Rosea" and "L-Theanine"
>
> THE SIDE BAND:
> - A steel blue / dark slate blue band wrapping the circumference
> - Divided into segments with: the "aire" logo in white, a "Calm Mint" label in a darker navy rectangle, "FIND YOUR BALANCE" in bold italic white uppercase, and "airepouches.com" in white
>
> OVERALL PALETTE: White lid, dark navy text, soft sky-blue/cyan accent on the wave mark, steel blue side band. Clean, premium, pharmaceutical-wellness aesthetic.

## TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

1. **Always attach the PDF** — Gemini needs to see the actual final label design every time
2. **If the shape is wrong** — add: "This is the same form factor as a Zyn or On! nicotine pouch can — a small flat round plastic container like a hockey puck, NOT a tall beverage can"
3. **If the label is wrong** — point Gemini specifically to the PDF and say "match the label in the attached image EXACTLY — every text element, every color, every position"
4. **Iterate** — first get the can right in isolation on white, THEN use that output as an additional reference for the lifestyle/scene shots
5. **Recommended workflow**: Generate Prompts 1A-1D first (clean product shots on white). Once you have good renders of the can, attach THOSE as additional reference images alongside the PDF for all subsequent lifestyle and Instagram prompts
